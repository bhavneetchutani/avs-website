# AVS truck study — source and proportion references

Researched and visually inspected 5 September 2026. These images are modeling references, not site photography or textures. The delivered truck is an original generic North American highway tractor, not an exact branded replica or a claim that AVS owns trucks.

## Tractor

- [Kenworth 76-inch mid-roof sleeper](https://www.kenworth.com/about-us/news/mid-roof-sleeper/): Kenworth specifically describes this configuration for flatbed and tanker work. The 76-inch designation converts to 1.9304 m. It offers 6.5 ft of interior headroom; that is not the overall vehicle height.
- [Kenworth T680 Next Generation launch](https://kenworth.com/about-us/news/the-kenworth-t680-next-generation-kenworth-launches-new-on-highway-flagship/): 125-inch BBC standard (3.175 m), 119-inch BBC MX (3.0226 m); optional 40-, 52- and 76-inch sleepers. BBC means bumper to back of cab, excluding the sleeper. Manufacturer describes a 38–42 inch trailer gap for tightly packaged configurations. Do not add BBC and nominal sleeper depth blindly: body boundaries and catalog sleeper labels can differ.
- [Peterbilt 579 manufacturer page](https://www.peterbilt.com/trucks/on-highway/579): complementary aero shape study; downloaded and inspected `peterbilt-579.png` from [manufacturer image](https://www.peterbilt.com/static-assets/images/truck/579%20and%20Day%20Cab%20Trucks.png). Low nose, sloping hood, grille slightly narrower at its base, curved front-fender crowns, inset headlamp assemblies, dark lower intake, broad lightly curved windshield and two wipers, two-stage mirrors, access steps, side-fairing panel breaks, baggage-door seams. Steer wheels have convex hubs; outer dual-drive wheel faces are more deeply dished. Front and drive axles need different wheel treatment.
- [Peterbilt Heavy Duty Body Builder Manual](https://www.peterbilt.com/static-assets/documents/resources/HD_Body_Builder_Manual_Rev_D.pdf), printed page 3-6 / PDF page 24: `peterbilt-579-dimensions.png`, inspected front/top/side orthographic. The shown **579-118** has 118 in BBC, 94 in overall body width (2.3876 m), 120.6 in mirror-to-mirror envelope (3.0632 m). These are another manufacturer's configuration and a proportion check, not T680 measurements.
- Same manual printed page 3-14 / PDF page 32: `peterbilt-sleeper-dimensions.png`. Sleeper external width illustrated at 94.5 in (2.4003 m); table distinguishes nominal sleeper model from actual external box length. Roof heights are referenced from **bottom of frame rail**, not the ground. Avoid misreading either as total vehicle height.
- `tractor.jpg` was separately inspected by the primary modeler: [2018 Kenworth T680 dealer photo](https://orion.soarr.com/photos/16430553/800x/2018-kenworth-t680.1.jpg).
- [Milestar BT818SW manufacturer specification](https://milestartires.com/medium-truck/trailer/bt818sw/): one 295/75R22.5 tire example has 39.9 in overall diameter (1.0135 m), 11.7 in section width (0.2972 m), and 22.5 in nominal wheel diameter (0.5715 m). Tire sizes vary; roughly 0.51 m tire radius is a sound scale check, not 22.5 inches for the entire tire.

## Flatbed

- [Fontaine Revolution brochure](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/60c307c7a91af077ec2b2b8d_Rev-Brochure.pdf), page 4: `fontaine-profiles.png`, visually inspected. 45-, 48- and 53-foot flatbeds shown. 53 ft = **16.1544 m**. Flatbed deck stays one level; step-deck drops behind the gooseneck. 53-foot dropdeck example has 11-foot upper / 42-foot lower deck. Wide-spread axle and closely spaced tandem variants both exist; the chosen model should be internally consistent.
- [Fontaine Revolution product details](https://fontainetrailer.com/fontaine-trailers/revolution-aluminum/): all-aluminum, unitized extruded floor and routed aluminum side rail; sliding winch track on both sides. The **Revolution has no exposed conventional crossmembers**. If modeling a steel I-beam/crossmember underside with timber deck strips, the Infinity combo below is the more appropriate construction reference.
- [Fontaine Infinity brochure](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/60c30e872778e700ab252b13_Infinity-Brochure.pdf), pages 2–3: steel mainbeams and crossbracing, aluminum floor/siderails/rear skirt, four full-length 1 1/8-inch apitong nailing strips. Mainbeam webs 25 inches deep in the main section; steel I-beam crossmembers at 12-inch centers. Mudflaps behind each axle. Useful construction reference for a traditional combo flatbed.
- `fontaine-flatbed-rear.png`: [manufacturer rear three-quarter image](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/641a0f4e29684115f9e202d1_Rev-Flat-rear-bolt-on-bumper-min-p-2000.png). Visually inspected: slight unloaded camber; 3 large red circular rear lights per side; three smaller center lamps; rubber dock bumpers at outer corners; silver underride crossbar; dual tires and wide hanging rubber mudflaps. Visible longitudinal brown strips in silver deck.
- `fontaine-rail.jpg`: [manufacturer RASR close-up](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/657cba2d9bac8ce6930bf7d9_RASR-Side-Rail.jpg). Visually inspected: small repeated rectangular stake pockets cut into the top of the outer rail; raised lip beside flush timber nailing strip; ribbed aluminum deck inboard. This is not a fence of tall posts.
- `fontaine-winch.jpg`: [manufacturer winch-track close-up](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/657cba701d62c7ee3e67b26d_Sliding-Winch-Track.jpg). Visually inspected: black steel hanging winches with ratchet wheels and transverse strap drums, under the outer rail. Conspicuity tape alternates red and white and sits above the winches; small amber markers interrupt it.

## Cargo

Existing AVS draft copy names wall panels, structural steel and precast. AVS research also documents welded wire and civil materials. National Wire is an existing customer, making these manufacturer references especially relevant. They demonstrate product appearance, not shipment history for any particular pictured item.

- [National Wire products](https://nationalwirellc.com/products/): engineered mesh, industrial mesh, bright basic wire, pencil rod, black annealed wire. Products include traffic rail, wall systems, struts, locking rods, paving mats, straight and cut wire.
- `national-wire-engineered-mesh.png`: [source](https://nationalwirellc.com/site/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Untitled-design-3-e1667504381856-1029x720-1.png). Inspected: long dark ribbed steel bars with bent sections and cross wires; irregular bright cut ends.
- `national-wire-industrial-mesh.jpg`: [source](https://nationalwirellc.com/site/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/industrial-mesh-scaled.jpg). Inspected: rigid flat stacked welded grids with regular rectangular openings, very dark steel, tiny bright welds; repeated wire layers readable at edges.
- `national-wire-pencil-rod.jpg`: [source](https://nationalwirellc.com/site/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/MicrosoftTeams-image-10-scaled.jpg). Inspected: long parallel rods bound in flattened round bundles using narrow ties. Bright silver cut ends contrast with darker rough ribbed lengths. This is distinctly different from structural steel coils.

## Modeling checks

1. Hood leads the tractor; sleeper borders the fifth-wheel area. A semi has one steer axle and two drive axles, with dual tires behind. The trailer contributes its own two axles and dual tires.
2. Fifth wheel sits on chassis above the drive group; trailer kingpin underside meets it. Landing legs must be raised while connected, with daylight below their feet. Red/blue air lines and an electrical line bridge tractor and trailer.
3. Give metal edges small bevels. Concentrate polygons on hood/fender silhouette, wheels, mirrors and securement; repeated bolts can be merged by material. A good silhouette and separate materials will survive website scale better than hidden engine internals.
4. Keep cargo, tractor and trailer separate named nodes for scrolling. Cargo swaps should carry their matching dunnage and securement; do not leave straps suspended in air after a load disappears.
5. References remain outside runtime imports. All geometry and finish for the website should be original; no manufacturer logos, marketing claims or reference-photo pixels need to ship in the GLB.
