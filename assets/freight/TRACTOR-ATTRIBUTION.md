# Peterbilt tractor attribution and provenance

“[1978 Peterbilt 359 Standard](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1978-peterbilt-359-standard-0599b3d7f9a5437fa905b20674d16d17)” by [hans1240](https://sketchfab.com/hans1240) is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The tractor used in the AVS Freight concept is an adaptation: its painted body panels use AVS blue (#2196E8), its other materials and sizing were adjusted, overlapping cab/sleeper border faces were removed, radiator fins and fifth-wheel mounting risers were added, and meshes were consolidated and compressed for website use. The adaptation has no added AVS logo or door lettering. No endorsement of AVS Freight or these changes by the creator is implied.
