Settlement

How we pay

Every term below is what we actually do, not a target. Ask a carrier who has run for us.

Net 30

Standard terms

From the day we have a complete invoice packet: signed rate confirmation, signed bills of lading, and any receipts you're claiming. Send it once and it's clean.

2 days

QuickPay, at 4%

Check issued within two business days of a complete packet, for a 4% fee on the invoice. Take it or leave it invoice by invoice. It isn't a standing election, and choosing it once doesn't sign you up for it.

NOA

Factored? Fine.

Most of what we pay out goes to factors, and we handle notices of assignment properly. We don't need to approve your factor and we won't hold payment waiting on a release letter from an old one.

Check

Company check, mailed

To the remit-to address on file, yours or your factor's. Keep that address current with us and nothing goes astray.

Detention, layover, TONU, stop-offs, tarping and driver assist are all paid on a published schedule. It's in the carrier packet, and the rate confirmation for your load restates whatever applies to it. Nothing gets invented at settlement.

Getting set up

What we need from you

One packet, once. We refresh it annually rather than asking you to re-send everything every quarter.

Send it to kk@avsfreight.com and Preet will come back to you.

  1. 01

    Operating authority and W-9

    Active MC or DOT authority in your legal name, and a W-9 that matches it.

  2. 02

    Certificate of insurance

    We require every carrier to carry at least $1,000,000 auto liability, $100,000 cargo, $1,000,000 general liability, and workers' compensation to statute. Name us as certificate holder and we'll take it direct from your agent.

  3. 03

    Signed broker–carrier agreement

    We'll send it to sign electronically. Read it — it's written to be read, and the accessorial schedule is an exhibit to it.

  4. 04

    Notice of assignment, if you factor

    Send your factor's NOA at setup, and tell us before you switch. That single message is the difference between getting paid on time and chasing a check to the wrong lockbox.

On the load

What the freight is actually like

It's tarped, and it's staged

Panels and steel go on in the order the crew wants them off, first piece needed loaded last. The shipper knows this and loads to it. You won't be re-stacking a trailer at the curb.

The delivery is a jobsite

No dock, no forklift, an appointment window, and PPE rules that change site to site. We tell you the rules before you accept, not when you arrive.

Weights are normal

Flatbed loads run close to 38,000 lb and top out at 48,000 lb — standard legal weight. Hotshot is lighter again. Everything we book runs at legal weight, on ordinary equipment.

One number, start to finish

Every load carries one reference number, and it's the same one on the rate confirmation, the paperwork and the check stub. When you call about it, whoever answers can pull it up.
